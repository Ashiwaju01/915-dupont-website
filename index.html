Same four walls, two very different hours. Pick one to explore.

☕ Coffee, 9–3:30
🥃 Bar, 5pm–2am

Mornings move slow here on purpose. Filter and espresso, good light through the front window, low music, and a room built for sitting still rather than turning tables fast.

Order at the counter and find a seat — the kitchen's already prepping for lunch service by mid-morning.

From The Bar

A short, rotating list — each one built around a record.

Every cocktail here is named for the album that inspired it. Ask the bartender what's playing.

Sombras

Altos blanco tequila · passion tomato · sancho pepper · agave · dill

Bright and savory — the tomato reads as summer, not tomato. Sancho pepper opens warm and floral, then dill lands cool and green.

Under Current

Lot 40 Canadian rye · house tea blend · sherry · black cardamom · strawberry foam

Rye and sherry hold a slow weight underneath. Black cardamom warms the finish, then strawberry comes in light, almost shy.

Clear

London dry gin · thai basil · pineapple · sichuan pepper

Green in the nose with a slow tingle at the back — bright and cool, built for a warm room.

Brown

Jameson · lychee · black tea · ume · pinch of salt

Whiskey holds the ground, tea and lychee open up on top, and a pinch of salt keeps it honest.
